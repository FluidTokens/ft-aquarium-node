Sorted by health factor ascending — closest to liquidation first. Loans that are only approaching the threshold are included on purpose: where the route is capital in advance, the principal has to be found before the loan goes sour, and a list of what is already liquidatable would arrive too late to act on.

A health factor below 1.0 is liquidatable now. Every figure comes from the same code the bot acts through — the scanner, the health service, the economics gate, the pool resolver and the pay-in-advance builder — so what is shown here is what the bot would decide. Where a value cannot be computed it reads unknown with the reason; it is never shown as zero.

No loans indexed. On a healthy node that means a quiet market — but it looks identical to a node pinned to a superseded deployment, or to one whose decoder has gone blind. Check the scan summary at /api/v1/loans/liquidations, and read three numbers together: bonds_scanned (bonds with a live loan), settled (bonds whose loan is gone) and unreadable. Both counts zero means nothing was found at all — an indexing problem, not a quiet market. And unreadable must be 0: while it is not, the settled count cannot be trusted either, because a loan that is present and merely illegible is counted as one that is gone.
